The idea

What separates truly successful agents from everyone else? It isn't talent. It's a system.

House Work is that system. Every client touchpoint from the first hello to a week past closing, handled, in your voice, on time. It streamlines your business and it gives you your time back.

What I do

Your client care lead, working inside your own tools.

Intake and setup

Welcome email, property questionnaire, deal folder, and a lifecycle board for every listing or purchase. Your clients hear from a real person on day one.

Listing prep and launch

I book and chase your inspector, stager, cleaner, and photographer, draft your write-up and fact sheet, prep the MLS data sheet, and send the "we're live" email with everything attached. Coming soon, just listed, and sold posts, ready for your feed.

While it's on the market

A weekly property update so your sellers keep a finger on the pulse and see the work you're doing. Tough conversations get easier when nothing comes as a surprise. Showing feedback relayed the same day.

Offers and conditions

An offer summary for you, the accepted-offer email for them, a condition tracker with reminders, and steady check-ins that keep everything on track.

Firm to close, and after

Touchpoints scheduled backwards from closing: revisits, utilities, moving date, keys, week-before, closing day. Then the week-after check-in, the review ask, and a clean hand-off to your long-term plan.

Your moments, on cue

I'll remind you when to drop off the moving kit, when a pop-by lands best, when a call from you would mean the most. You show up; I make sure you never miss the moment.

Buyers too. Same system, from consult to keys.
