The system

From green light to paid out, nothing gets dropped.

Every deal runs on its own lifecycle board. On the seller side that's 27 stages and roughly 190 steps, each with a date and an owner. Here's the short version.

  1. Green light

    You've got a signed client. Welcome email, questionnaire, deal folder, board built, group chat open. They hear from a real person on day one.

  2. Listing prep

    Your pre-listing plan, run to the letter: stager, photographer, cleaner, inspector, whatever you use. Booked, confirmed, chased. Inspection report read and items flagged for your review.

  3. Collateral

    Write-up drafted. Listing details packaged up front, in your brand, so nobody has to call and ask. MLS data sheet and attachments ready for input.

  4. Launch

    Listing paperwork out for signature, show-ready tips, the "we're live" email, showing instructions, coming soon and just listed posts.

  5. On the market

    The weekly property update, so your sellers see the work and nothing comes as a surprise. Open house coordination. Price change communication.

  6. Offers

    Offer summary for you, presentation logistics, then the accepted-offer email and deposit instructions for them. Everyone knows what happens next.

  7. Conditions

    A tracker with due-date reminders two days out and the morning of, for you and for your clients. "The waiting is hard" check-ins along the way.

  8. Firm to close

    Revisits, utilities, moving date, keys, the week before, closing day, all scheduled backwards from closing. Lawyer and FINTRAC package prepped.

  9. Paid out

    Happy closing day. Sold post. Then I close the file: your database updated with their new address and past-client status, and a clean hand-off to your long-term plan.

Buyers run the same way: consult, search, offer, conditions, firm to close, keys, file closed.

FAQ

Questions agents ask me.

Are you client facing?

Yes. Your clients hear from a real person, introduced as client care for your team, under your name and your brand. You're still the agent. I'm the follow-through.

I don't have a database or a system yet.

No problem. I'll help you get your database set up, and we run my system, tweaked to match your brand and the way you talk to people. You don't have to build anything.

I don't need the whole program. What are my options?

Let's talk. One-off projects and single tools are on the table: a weekly update your sellers actually read, a closing countdown for your buyers, a lifecycle board you run yourself. Tell me where it hurts and we'll scope it.

What does it cost?

It depends on the size of your business and the number of transactions you do. What I can tell you now: it's certainly less than a full-time assistant on your payroll. We'll put real numbers on it in the discovery call.

What are some examples of what you send to clients?

The welcome email on day one. The "we're live" email with the listing details attached. A weekly property update with the showing stats and your read on them. The accepted-offer email that explains what happens next. Condition due-date reminders. The closing countdown: revisits, utilities, movers, keys. Happy closing day. Ask on the call and I'll walk you through a real sequence.

Will it sound like me?

That's the whole point. You tell me the vibe of your brand and how you talk to people, and I write to that. I read every message before it goes out. If it doesn't sound like you on your best day, it doesn't go.

Do you show up at the property?

No. House Work is virtual. Showings, signs, lockboxes, and drop-offs stay with you or your vendor. I coordinate them so they happen on time, and I chase whoever needs chasing.

Does this work for buyers?

Yes. Same system, from consult to keys: showing messages, weekly check-ins, the inspection countdown, condition tracking, the closing sequence, and the hand-off to your database.

What do you need from me?

Access to your tools, a feel for your brand and how you like to talk to people, and a green light on each deal. After that I run it, keep my eyes on everything, and you add your insight where it matters.

How do we start?

Book a discovery call. Thirty minutes, no pitch deck. If it fits, we plan the setup together. Getting integrated into your tools and your brand takes a little time up front, and it's worth doing properly.
